ENROLLED HOUSE BILL No. 4189 Act No. 65 Public Acts of 2026 Approved by the Governor July 21, 2026 Filed with the Secretary of State July 23, 2026 EFFECTIVE DATE: July 23, 2026 state of michigan 103rd Legislature Regular session of 2026 Introduced by Reps. Woolford, Kelly, Beson, Cavitt, Wozniak, Pavlov, Smit, Johnsen, Bierlein, DeSana, Maddock, Schuette, Robinson, Schmaltz, Kunse, Linting, DeBoyer, Hoadley, Fairbairn, Fox, Martin, Frisbie, Outman and Aragona ENROLLED HOUSE BILL No. 4189 AN ACT to amend 2001 PA 142, entitled An act to consolidate prior acts naming certain Michigan highways; to provide for the naming of certain highways; to prescribe certain duties of the state transportation department; and to repeal acts and parts of acts and certain resolutions, (MCL 250.1001 to 250.2092) by adding section 1051. The People of the State of Michigan enact: Sec. 1051. (1) The bridge on South Grand Avenue over highway I-96 in the village of Fowlerville shall be known as the LCpl Michael W. Hanks Memorial Bridge. (2) Subject to section 2, a sign designating the location of the LCpl Michael W. Hanks Memorial Bridge must be placed at the approach of the bridge on highway I-96. (3) The state transportation department shall coordinate with the Livingston County road commission in placing an appropriate sign designating the location of the LCpl Michael W. Hanks Memorial Bridge at each approach of the bridge on South Grand Avenue. This act is ordered to take immediate effect.
