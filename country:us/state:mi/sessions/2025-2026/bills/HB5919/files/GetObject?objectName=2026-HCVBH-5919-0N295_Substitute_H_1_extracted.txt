21 Sec. 562. (1) The learner-first district is created and shall
22 be governed by this part for the following purposes:
23 (a) To provide innovative, flexible, transparent, safe,
24 efficient, and effective public educational services throughout the
25 state.
26 (b) To provide parents and legal guardians with greater public
27 educational choices and an increased ability to determine and
28 direct the care, teaching, and education of their children.
29 (c) To advance new flexible and adaptable educational models,

1 including the expanded use of technology in education, online
2 learning, and the provision of mastery-based learning.
3 (d) To stimulate innovation in teaching methods, including the
4 use of computer-adaptive methods of instruction.
5 (e) To provide new professional opportunities for teachers
6 within the public education system.
7 (f) To create new public-private partnerships for the
8 provision of public educational services.
9 (2) All of the following apply to the learner-first district
10 created under this section:
11 (a) The learner-first district is a school district for the
12 purposes of section 11 of article IX of the state constitution of
13 1963.

15 and general supervision of the state board of education under
16 section 3 of article VIII of the state constitution of 1963.
17 (c) The learner-first district is a political subdivision and
18 public body corporate separate and distinct from this state and
19 other school districts in this state.
20 (d) The learner-first district and members of the learner-
21 first district school board, its superintendent, administrators,
22 officers, employees, and volunteers have governmental immunity
23 under section 7 of 1964 PA 170, MCL 691.1407.
24 (e) The learner-first district encompasses a geographic area
25 containing the entirety of this state.
