HOUSE BILL NO. 6256

A bill to amend 1951 PA 51, entitled

"An act to provide for the classification of all public roads, streets, and highways in this state, and for the revision of that classification and for additions to and deletions from each classification; to set up and establish the Michigan transportation fund; to provide for the deposits in the Michigan transportation fund of specific taxes on motor vehicles and motor vehicle fuels; to provide for the allocation of funds from the Michigan transportation fund and the use and administration of the fund for transportation purposes; to promote safe and efficient travel for motor vehicle drivers, bicyclists, pedestrians, and other legal users of roads, streets, and highways; to set up and establish the truck safety fund; to provide for the allocation of funds from the truck safety fund and administration of the fund for truck safety purposes; to set up and establish the Michigan truck safety commission; to establish certain standards for road contracts for certain businesses; to provide for the continuing review of transportation needs within the state; to authorize the state transportation commission, counties, cities, and villages to borrow money, issue bonds, and make pledges of funds for transportation purposes; to authorize counties to advance funds for the payment of deficiencies necessary for the payment of bonds issued under this act; to provide for the limitations, payment, retirement, and security of the bonds and pledges; to provide for appropriations and tax levies by counties and townships for county roads; to authorize contributions by townships for county roads; to provide for the establishment and administration of the state trunk line fund, local bridge fund, comprehensive transportation fund, and certain other funds; to provide for the deposits in the state trunk line fund, critical bridge fund, comprehensive transportation fund, and certain other funds of money raised by specific taxes and fees; to provide for definitions of public transportation functions and criteria; to define the purposes for which Michigan transportation funds may be allocated; to provide for Michigan transportation fund grants; to provide for review and approval of transportation programs; to provide for submission of annual legislative requests and reports; to provide for the establishment and functions of certain advisory entities; to provide for conditions for grants; to provide for the issuance of bonds and notes for transportation purposes; to provide for the powers and duties of certain state and local agencies and officials; to provide for the making of loans for transportation purposes by the state transportation department and for the receipt and repayment by local units and agencies of those loans from certain specified sources; to investigate and study the tolling of roads, streets, highways, or bridges; and to repeal acts and parts of acts,"

by amending sections 10 and 11 (MCL 247.660 and 247.661), section 10 as amended by 2022 PA 50 and section 11 as amended by 2015 PA 175, and by adding section 11k.

(iv) Test installations of innovative warning devices or other innovative applications.

(v) Construction of new grade separations.

(vi) A cash incentive payment made pursuant to under subsection (1)(c)(iv) for any public road or street crossing, in an amount no greater than the cost of installing flashing light signals and half roadway gates at the crossing.

(vii) Any other work that would be eligible for funding under the federal railroad-highway grade crossing improvement program or other comparable programs.

(c) "Transportation economic development fund" means the transportation economic development fund created in section 2 of 1987 PA 231, MCL 247.902.

Sec. 11k. (1) The local agency disaster relief board fund is created in the state treasury. The state treasurer shall deposit money and other assets received from any source in the fund. The state treasurer shall direct the investment of money in the fund and credit interest and earnings from the investments to the fund. The department is the administrator of the fund for audits of the fund.

(2) The board shall expend money appropriated to the fund and the interest accruing to the fund as provided in this section. Up to $5,000,000.00 annually may be deposited in the fund and the balance of the fund must not exceed $50,000,000.00 at the beginning of a fiscal year plus funds already obligated but not spent. The money in the fund is not subject to section 12(15).

(3) The local agency disaster relief program is created in the department. The program must provide financial assistance as provided in this section and in accordance with the board's policies, criteria, agreements, or contracts to assist in repairing or replacing a local road agency's infrastructure following a local or state-level declared emergency.

(4) The local agency disaster relief board is created in the department. The first members of the board must be appointed by not later than the first October 1 after the effective date of the amendatory act that added this section. The board must, except as otherwise provided in this section, consist of the all of the following individuals:

(a) Three voting members appointed by the County Road Association of Michigan.

(b) Two voting members appointed by the Michigan Municipal League.

(c) One voting member, who must be an engineer, appointed jointly by the County Road Association of Michigan and the Michigan Municipal League.

(d) One nonvoting member appointed by the department.

(e) One nonvoting member appointed by the department of environment, Great Lakes, and energy.

(f) One nonvoting member appointed by the department of state police.

(5) Except as provided in subsection (6), members of the board shall serve for terms of 2 years or until a successor is appointed, whichever is later.

(6) Of the first members appointed to the board, the following members shall serve a term of 3 years:

(a) Two voting members appointed under subsection (4)(a) as determined by the County Road Association of Michigan.

(b) One voting member appointed under subsection (4)(b) as determined by the Michigan Municipal League.

(c) The nonvoting member appointed under subsection (4)(d).

(7) At the expiration of a member's term or if a vacancy occurs on the board, an individual must be appointed to the board or to fill the vacancy for the remainder of the term subject to all of the following:

(a) A successor to a voting member appointed under subsection (4)(a), (b), or (c) must be appointed jointly by the County Road Association of Michigan and the Michigan Municipal League.

(b) A successor to a nonvoting member appointed under subsection (4)(d), (e), or (f) must be appointed by the same department that appointed that nonvoting member.

(8) A member of the board may be removed by the governor or the board for incompetence, dereliction of duty, malfeasance, misfeasance, nonfeasance in office, or any other good cause.

(9) The nonvoting member appointed by the department under subsection (4)(d) shall call the first meeting of the board. At the first meeting, the board shall elect from among the board's voting members a chairperson and other officers that the board considers necessary or appropriate. After the first meeting, the board shall meet at least quarterly. The chairperson may call a special meeting of the board in response to a local or state-level declared emergency that is of a fiscal magnitude established as actionable by the board.

(10) A majority of the voting members of the board constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at a meeting of the board. A majority of the voting members present and serving is required for official action of the board.

(11) A member of the board shall serve without compensation but may receive reimbursement for necessary travel and expenses consistent with applicable law and the rules and procedures of the civil service commission and department of technology, management, and budget or local road agency policies, subject to available funding.

(12) The board shall conduct its business in compliance with the open meetings act, 1976 PA 267, MCL 15.261 to 15.275.

(13) A writing prepared, owned, used, in the possession of, or retained by the board in the performance of an official function is subject to the freedom of information act, 1976 PA 442, MCL 15.231 to 15.246.

(14) The board may employ part-time or full-time managers or engineers and may contract with a person, in accordance with the board's policies, to provide expertise or to perform professional or technical services, administrative assistance, or legal counsel in accordance with the board's policies. The board shall determine the duties of a manager, engineer, or other person employed under this subsection and shall require the manager, engineer, or other person to maintain all necessary insurances.

(15) The program must provide funding to local road agencies for 1 or more of the following:

(a) Restoring or replacing a public local road, bridge, or other physical transportation infrastructure to the condition existing before the state-level declared emergency event or to the current state and federal standards as recognized in board policy, if the local road agency has exhausted all other federal and state funding sources.

(b) Completing engineering and design to meet current standards.

(c) Purchasing land or construction easements required to construct the public road, bridge, or physical transportation infrastructure.

(d) Conducting inspection and oversight necessary to ensure that performance standards are met.

(e) Performing project administration necessary to ensure that financial accountability standards are met.

(16) The board may approve the use of money in the fund for other activities, including, but not limited to, items not otherwise eligible for funding from a federal or state disaster program or from federal or state matching funds, needed to restore a public road, a bridge, drainage, or other facilities as permitted by law on a demonstrated need by a local road agency.

(17) To obtain funding under subsection (15), a local road agency must file a funding application with the board at any time as determined by the board, on a form approved by the board that contains the information required by the board.

(18) The board shall establish policies, criteria, definitions, and timelines for funding a repair or replace work project and a review process for considering funding applications. By not later than 180 days after receiving a funding application, the board shall notify the applicant in writing whether the funding application is approved in part or in entirety, or if the funding application is rejected or remains pending for a specific reason. Before releasing money from the program, the board shall enter into an agreement with the funding recipient.

(19) For each year in which the board receives funding applications under subsection (17), the board shall report by October 1 to the standing committees of the senate and house of representatives with primary jurisdiction over transportation issues and to the senate and house of representatives appropriation subcommittees on transportation on the utilization of money from the fund. The report must include, at a minimum, all of the following:

(a) The number of funding applications received.

(b) The name of each local road agency that submitted a funding application and whether each funding application was approved or denied.

(c) The amount of local match for each funding application that was granted.

(d) The individual and annual cumulative amount of money awarded under this section, including an identification of the purpose of the money awarded.

(20) As used in this section:

(a) "Board" means the local agency disaster relief board created in subsection (4).

(b) "Fund" means the local agency disaster relief board fund created in subsection (1).

(c) "Funding application" means an application under subsection (17) for money from the program.

(d) "Local road agency" means that term as defined in section 9a.

(e) "Program" means the local agency disaster relief program created in subsection (3).
