HOUSE BILL NO. 6268

A bill to amend 1961 PA 236, entitled

"Revised judicature act of 1961,"

by amending section 1621 (MCL 600.1621), as amended by 1995 PA 161.

the people of the state of michigan enact:

Sec. 1621. (1) Except for actions Subject to subsections (2) and (3), and except as provided for in sections 1605, 1611, 1615, and 1629, and 1635, venue is determined as follows:

(a) The county in which a defendant resides, has a place of business, or conducts business, or in which the registered office of a defendant corporation is located, the original cause of action arises and to which 1 or more of the following apply is a proper county in which to commence and try an action: .

(i) A defendant resides in the county.

(ii) A defendant has a place of business in the county.

(iii) A defendant conducts business in the county.

(iv) A defendant is a corporation and has a registered office in the county.

(b) If none of the defendants meet 1 or more of the county in which the original cause of action arises does not meet at least 1 of the criteria in conditions listed under subdivision (a), the county in which a plaintiff resides or has a place of business, or in which the registered office of a plaintiff corporation is located, original cause of action arises and to which 1 or more of the following apply is a proper county in which to commence and try an action: .

(i) A plaintiff resides in the county.

(ii) A plaintiff has a place of business in the county.

(iii) A plaintiff conducts business in the county.

(iv) A plaintiff is a corporation and has a registered office in the county.

(c) If the county in which the original cause of action arises does not meet at least 1 of the conditions listed under subdivision (a) or (b), a county to which both of the following apply is a proper county in which to commence and try an action:

(i) One or more of the following apply:

(A) A plaintiff resides in the county.

(B) A plaintiff has a place of business in the county.

(C) A plaintiff conducts business in the county.

(D) A plaintiff is a corporation and has a registered office in the county.

(ii) One or more of the following apply:

(A) A defendant resides in the county.

(B) A defendant has a place of business in the county.

(C) A defendant conducts business in the county.

(D) A defendant is a corporation and has a registered office in the county.

(d) If no county meets the conditions under subdivision (a), (b), or (c), a county that meets the conditions under section 1627 is a proper county in which to commence and try an action.

(2) (c) An action against a fiduciary appointed by court order shall must be commenced in the county in which the fiduciary was appointed.

(3) A party may file a motion to change venue based on hardship or inconvenience.

(4) For the purposes of this section, a county in which a defendant conducts business includes, but is not limited to, a county in which the defendant's product is sold at retail.
