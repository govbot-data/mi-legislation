HOUSE BILL NO. 6309 A bill to amend 2001 PA 142, entitled "Michigan memorial highway act," (MCL 250.1001 to 250.2092) by adding section 116. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 116. The portion of highway M-86 in Branch County beginning at the intersection of highway M-86 and highway US-12 and continuing west to the intersection of highway M-86 and Arbogast Road shall be known as the "Casey Rice Memorial Highway".
