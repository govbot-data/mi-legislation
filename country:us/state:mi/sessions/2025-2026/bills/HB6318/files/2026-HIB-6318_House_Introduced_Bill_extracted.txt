HOUSE BILL NO. 6318 A bill to amend 1954 PA 116, entitled "Michigan election law," (MCL 168.1 to 168.992) by adding section 492c. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 492c. The right of citizens of this state to vote shall not be denied or abridged on account of sex.
