HOUSE BILL NO. 6394 A bill to amend 1971 PA 227, entitled "An act to prescribe the rights and duties of parties to home solicitation sales; to regulate certain telephone solicitation; to provide for the powers and duties of certain state officers and entities; and to prescribe penalties and remedies," (MCL 445.111 to 445.117) by adding section 1f. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 1f. If a provision of this act conflicts with a provision of the solicitation and oversight partnership act, the provision of the solicitation and oversight partnership act controls. Enacting section 1. This amendatory act does not take effect unless House Bill No. 6393 (request no. H06828'26) of the 103rd Legislature is enacted into law.
