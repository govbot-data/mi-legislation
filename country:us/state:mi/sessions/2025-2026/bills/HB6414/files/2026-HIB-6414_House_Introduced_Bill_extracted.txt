HOUSE BILL NO. 6414 A bill to amend 1954 PA 116, entitled "Michigan election law," (MCL 168.1 to 168.992) by adding section 29a. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 29a. A county, city, or township clerk may investigate, or cause to be investigated, any alleged violation of the election laws or regulations by the secretary of state, the bureau of elections, the director of elections, or any staff member in either the secretary of state's office or the bureau of elections, and report that alleged violation to the attorney general or appropriate prosecuting attorney for further investigation and enforcement.
