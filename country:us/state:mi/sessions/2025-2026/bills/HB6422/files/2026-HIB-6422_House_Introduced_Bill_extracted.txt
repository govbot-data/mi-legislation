HOUSE BILL NO. 6422 A bill to amend 1976 PA 451, entitled "The revised school code," (MCL 380.1 to 380.1852) by adding section 1238. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 1238. A school district, an intermediate school district, or a public school academy shall annually notify each of its pupils and a parent or legal guardian of each of its pupils that all pupils may attend a religious instruction class, as described in section 1561(3)(d).
