HOUSE BILL NO. 6432

A bill to amend 2011 PA 152, entitled

"Publicly funded health insurance contribution act,"

(MCL 15.561 to 15.569) by adding section 7a.

the people of the state of michigan enact:

Sec. 7a. (1) A member of the state house of representatives or state senate shall comply with all of the following:

(a) Any reporting requirements for individuals enrolled in the Healthy Michigan plan.

(b) Not later than January 1, 2027, any community engagement activities required for individuals enrolled in the Healthy Michigan plan.

(2) If a member of the state house of representatives or state senate does not comply with subsection (1), a public employer shall not pay any costs for a medical benefit plan for that member until that member complies with subsection (1).

(3) As used in this section, "Healthy Michigan plan" means that term as defined in section 20161 of the public health code, 1978 PA 368, MCL 333.20161.
