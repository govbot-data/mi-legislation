HOUSE RESOLUTION NO. 369 HOUSE RESOLUTION NO. 369 Reps. Conlin, Foreman, Tsernoglou, Rheingans, Morgan, Glanville, Steckloff, Byrnes, Martus, Hope, Paiz, Miller, Arbit, Pohutsky, Brixie, Skaggs, Breen, Koleszar, Longjohn, MacDonell, Rogers and Weiss offered the following resolution: A resolution to declare September 21-25, 2026, as PFAS Awareness Week in the state of Michigan. Whereas, Michigan residents depend on clean and reliable drinking water, and protecting Michigan's water resources is important to the health and well-being of our communities; and Whereas, More than one million Michigan residents rely on private drinking water wells for their water supply; and Whereas, Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances, commonly known as PFAS, are a group of human-made chemicals that have been used in a variety of industrial and consumer applications and have been detected in water, soil, fish, wildlife, and other areas of Michigan's environment; and Whereas, As of March 2026, PFAS contamination had been identified at 340 sites in Michigan where the source of groundwater contamination was known; and Whereas, PFAS investigations have included industrial sites, landfills, airports, military installations, and other locations where PFAS may have been used or released; and Whereas, Since 2018, more than 7,500 samples have been collected from private and commercial drinking water wells as part of Michigan's PFAS investigations, providing information to residents and communities about local water quality; and Whereas, Michigan routinely tests fish for PFAS and other contaminants and provides residents with Eat Safe Fish guidance to help them make informed decisions about consuming locally caught fish; and Whereas, Michigan continues to monitor surface water, groundwater, drinking water, fish, and other environmental resources to better understand the presence and potential sources of PFAS; and Whereas, Public awareness and access to reliable information can help Michigan residents make informed decisions regarding their drinking water, recreational activities, and consumption of locally caught fish; now, therefore, be it Resolved by the House of Representatives, That the members of this legislative body declare September 21-25, 2026, as PFAS Awareness Week in the state of Michigan; and be it further Resolved, That we encourage Michigan residents to learn more about PFAS, and take appropriate action when drinking water or eating caught fish; and be it further Resolved, That copies of this resolution be transmitted to the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy, the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services, and the Michigan PFAS Action Response Team.
