HOUSE RESOLUTION NO. 371

Reps. B. Carter, Rheingans, Dievendorf, Edwards, Breen, Steckloff, Foreman, MacDonell and Myers-Phillips offered the following resolution:

A resolution urging the Michigan Board of Education to advise Michigan schools to continue referring to the Great Lakes as Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, and Ontario.
