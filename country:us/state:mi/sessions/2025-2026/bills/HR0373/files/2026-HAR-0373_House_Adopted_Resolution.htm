HOUSE RESOLUTION NO. 373

Reps. DeBoyer, Fox, Kelly and Rigas offered the following resolution:

A resolution to condemn socialism, the platform of the Democratic Socialists for America, and politicians who espouse socialist views, and to oppose the implementation of socialist policies in the state of Michigan and the United States.

Whereas, Socialist ideology calls for the consolidation of power within government, and in doing so gives more power to the state than the people, and

Whereas, Socialism and communism perpetuate the inaccurate belief that the government can make better decisions than people can make for themselves, and

Whereas, The United States and Michigan, as democratic republics, were founded on the principle that the power of the government is derived from the people, and the policies suggested by socialist organizations throughout the country go vehemently against the core principles of this state and nation; and

Whereas, With the recent rise in political candidates self-proclaimed as Democratic Socialists, the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) and other activist organizations seek to grow their coalition to undermine the primary principles of this state and country; and

Whereas, Now, in 2026, there are at least 282 DSA-endorsed candidates throughout the country running for positions at every level of government, and the number increases when factoring in those who were not endorsed by the organization, but are self-proclaimed Democratic Socialists; and

Whereas, These endorsed and self-proclaimed Democratic Socialist politicians promote socialist ideals in ways that mislead voters about the true agenda that they are supporting; and

Whereas, The DSA call for abolishing the United States Senate and replacing the President and Supreme Court with an executive and judiciary chosen by and subordinate to Congress; and
