HOUSE RESOLUTION NO. 373 HOUSE RESOLUTION NO. 373 Reps. DeBoyer, Fox, Kelly and Rigas offered the following resolution: A resolution to condemn socialism, the platform of the Democratic Socialists for America, and politicians who espouse socialist views, and to oppose the implementation of socialist policies in the state of Michigan and the United States. Whereas, Socialist ideology calls for the consolidation of power within government, and in doing so gives more power to the state than the people, and Whereas, Socialism and communism perpetuate the inaccurate belief that the government can make better decisions than people can make for themselves, and Whereas, The United States and Michigan, as democratic republics, were founded on the principle that the power of the government is derived from the people, and the policies suggested by socialist organizations throughout the country go vehemently against the core principles of this state and nation; and Whereas, With the recent rise in political candidates self-proclaimed as Democratic Socialists, the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) and other activist organizations seek to grow their coalition to undermine the primary principles of this state and country;
