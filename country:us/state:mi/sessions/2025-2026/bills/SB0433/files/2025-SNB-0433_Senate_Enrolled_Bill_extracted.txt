(1) By not later than July 1, 2027, the department of health and human services shall develop, and provide to the department, an informational notice in English, Spanish, and Arabic, containing all of the following: (a) Information on the dangers of tobacco products, vapor products, alternative nicotine products, THC cannabis products, and synthetic intoxicating hemp. (2) The department of health and human services shall annually update the informational notice developed under subsection (1) to account for any changes in health education or medical research related to the dangers of tobacco products, vapor products, alternative nicotine products, THC cannabis products, or synthetic intoxicating hemp, and shall provide the updated notice to the department by not later than July 1 of each year. (5) Beginning with the 2027-2028 school year, the board of a school district or intermediate school district, board of directors of a public school academy, or governing body of a nonpublic school shall post a link to the department webpage described in subsection (3) on the website of the school district, intermediate school district, public school academy, or nonpublic school.
