SENATE BILL NO. 1143 A bill to amend 2001 PA 142, entitled "Michigan memorial highway act," (MCL 250.1001 to 250.2092) by adding section 8d. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 8d. The portion of highway US-2 between the Michigan State Police Iron Mountain post and the junction of highway US-2 and highway M-95 shall be known as the "Trooper Carl Lindberg Memorial Highway".
