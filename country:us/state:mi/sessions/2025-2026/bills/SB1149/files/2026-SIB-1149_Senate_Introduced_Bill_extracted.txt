SENATE BILL NO. 1149 A bill to amend 1956 PA 218, entitled "The insurance code of 1956," (MCL 500.100 to 500.8302) by adding section 3406ww. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 3406ww. An insurer that delivers, issues for delivery, or renews in this state a health insurance policy shall provide coverage for a cervical, thoracic, and lumbar epidural steroid injection for acute radiculopathy with chronic pain that impacts the quality of life of an individual as determined by a provider without requiring any additional conditions for coverage.
