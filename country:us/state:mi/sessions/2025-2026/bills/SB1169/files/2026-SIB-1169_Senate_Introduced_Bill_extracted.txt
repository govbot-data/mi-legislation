SENATE BILL NO. 1169 A bill to amend 1961 PA 236, entitled "Revised judicature act of 1961," by amending section 531 (MCL 600.531). the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 531. The thirtieth judicial circuit consists of the county of Ingham and has 7 judges. Subject to section 550, this judicial circuit may have 1 additional judge beginning January 1, 2027.
