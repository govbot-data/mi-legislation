(21) (20) As used in this section, "financial assistance" means grants, loans, other economic assistance, and any other incentives or assistance under this act. CHAPTER 8F Sec. 90t. (1) The legislature finds all of the following: (a) Mass timber is rapidly emerging as an efficient, high-quality, low-carbon, modern building technology that relies on renewable, regionally sourced materials. (b) Mass timber products enable large-scale commercial, institutional, industrial, and multifamily buildings to be constructed primarily from engineered wood. (c) This state has significant advantages for mass timber adoption, including all of the following: (i) Large, sustainably managed forest resources. (ii) A strong forest products industry. (iii) A growing interest in sustainable construction and carbon reduction. (iv) A strategic location that allows this state to become a mass timber manufacturing hub in the Midwest. (d) Although there is significant growing interest and uptake, adoption of mass timber in this state remains limited because of several barriers, including all of the following: (i) Higher upfront costs compared to conventional materials. (ii) Limited familiarity with mass timber financing among lenders. (iii) Lack of mass timber production in the region. (iv) Developer risk and lack of familiarity during early market adoption. (e) A revolving loan fund could reduce financial barriers and accelerate market transformation, which would benefit this state by doing the following: (i) Increasing the number of mass timber buildings in this state. (ii) Supporting Michigan forest products economies, including in rural communities. (iii) Creating new markets for Michigan-grown timber and value-added wood manufacturing. (iv) Reducing carbon emissions. (v) Stimulating job creation in forestry, manufacturing, fabrication, construction, engineering, and architecture. (f) The activities authorized under this chapter to provide financing for, and promote the use of, mass timber are a public purpose and of paramount concern in the interest of the health, safety, and general welfare of the citizens of this state. (2) It is the intent of the legislature that the economic benefits and creation of jobs resulting from this chapter accrue substantially within this state. Sec. 90u. As used in this chapter: (a) "Eligible applicant" means any of the following: (i) A real estate developer or project owner. (ii) A nonprofit housing organization. (iii) A tribal government. (iv) An institution of higher education. (v) A municipality. (vi) A construction firm. (vii) A company manufacturing or fabricating, or seeking to manufacture or fabricate, mass timber. (b) "Institution of higher education" means that term as defined in section 88a. (c) "Loan program" means the mass timber loan program created under section 90v. (d) "Mass timber" means a type of building component or system of engineered wood building construction materials comprising layers of dimensional lumber or veneers, laminated together using adhesives or mechanical methods, including all of the following: (i) Cross-laminated timber. (ii) Nail-laminated timber. (iii) Glue-laminated timber. (iv) Dowel-laminated timber. (v) Structural composite lumber. (vi) Wood-concrete composites. (vii) Mass plywood and mass veneer panels. (e) "Mass timber advisory board" or "advisory board" means the mass timber advisory board created by section 90y. (f) "Mass timber building" means a building or structure with 1 or more of its main structural systems, such as the floors, walls, or roof, utilizing mass timber products. For purposes of this subdivision, a structural system does not utilize mass timber products unless not less than 80% of the portion of that structural system above the finished grade includes mass timber components. For purposes of determining whether the 80% requirement is met, that portion of the structural system below the finished grade is not included. (g) "Mass timber construction project" means a project to construct, restore, alter, renovate, expand, or improve a building or structure that incorporates mass timber in such a manner that the resulting building or structure qualifies as a mass timber building under subdivision (f). (h) "Mass timber revolving loan fund" means the Michigan mass timber revolving loan fund created in section 90x. (i) "Municipality" means that term as defined in section 5 of the local community stabilization authority act, 2014 PA 86, MCL 123.1345. (j) "Technical assistance program" means the mass timber technical assistance program created under section 90w. Sec. 90v. (1) The fund shall create the mass timber loan program to provide loans to eligible applicants to support mass timber production and the use of mass timber for construction in this state as described in this section. The fund shall use money transferred from the mass timber revolving loan fund or money appropriated to the loan program to make the loans. The loan program shall be operated and administered by the authorized officers, employees, and agents of the fund, including the MEDC and its employees. The mass timber advisory board shall advise and assist the fund with respect to the loan program. (2) The loan program must offer the following types of loans: (a) Pre-development loans for early-stage work for a mass timber construction project, including, but not limited to, feasibility studies, structural engineering, architectural design, and cost analysis. (b) Construction gap financing for construction activities for a mass timber construction project, including, but not limited to, procurement of mass timber components, structural systems, and specialized engineering. (c) Manufacturing and supply chain loans to support mass timber manufacturing facilities, mass timber fabrication equipment, mass timber supply chain expansion, and wood processing facilities. (3) The fund shall develop a detailed application, approval, and compliance process that is published and available on the website of the fund or the MEDC. The detailed application, approval, and compliance process must, at a minimum, provide for all of the following: (a) An eligible applicant may apply for a loan under the loan program. (b) The eligible applicant must use the loan for the purposes described in subsection (2). (c) The principal amount of a loan for a mass timber construction project under subsection (2)(a) or (b) must not exceed the lesser of 15% of the total hard construction costs of the mass timber construction project or $15,000,000.00. The total hard construction costs must be documented by the eligible applicant and verified by the fund. As used in this subdivision, "hard construction costs" means the direct costs of labor, materials, equipment, and site work necessary to construct, restore, alter, renovate, or improve the building or structure, and does not include soft costs such as architectural and engineering fees, legal fees, financing costs, permit fees, developer fees, or land acquisition costs. (d) If the loan is for a mass timber construction project, the eligible applicant must submit a development plan that includes all of the following information: (i) A legal description of the property to which the plan applies. (ii) The designation of boundaries of the property to which the plan applies in relation to highways, streets, or otherwise. (iii) The location and extent of existing streets and other public facilities in the vicinity of the property to which the plan applies. (iv) The location, character, and extent of the categories of public and private land uses then existing and proposed for the property to which the plan applies, including residential, recreational, commercial, industrial, educational, and other uses. (v) A description of the proposed construction, restoration, alteration, renovation, or improvement of a building or structure, including the proposed use of mass timber. (vi) A statement of the stages of development planned and the estimated time of completion of each stage. (vii) An estimate of the cost of the development, a statement of the proposed method of financing the development, and the ability of the applicant to arrange the financing required in addition to the loan under the loan program. (viii) Designation of the person or persons to whom all or a portion of the development is to be leased, sold, or conveyed in any manner and for whose benefit the project is being undertaken if that information is available to the applicant. (ix) If the information described in subparagraph (viii) is not available to the applicant, the procedures for bidding for the leasing, purchasing, or conveying in any manner of all or a portion of the development on its completion. (e) The eligible applicant must pay interest on the loan at a rate determined by the fund, which must not exceed the adjusted prime rate minus 1 percentage point and must not be less than the adjusted prime rate minus 3 percentage points. As used in this subdivision, "adjusted prime rate" means that term as defined in and determined under section 23 of 1941 PA 122, MCL 205.23. (f) Except as otherwise provided in subdivision (g), the term of the loan must not be less than 3 years or more than 15 years. (g) On request of the applicant, the fund shall provide a grace period of not more than 24 months for repayment of the loan. (4) Before approving an application under this section, the fund shall consider and document, at a minimum, all of the following criteria: (a) For a loan for a mass timber construction project, all of the following: (i) Whether the project is a mass timber building. (ii) Whether the project will result in significant carbon reduction. (iii) Whether the project will source wood from sustainably managed forests. (iv) Whether the project will support the development of mass timber manufacturing, fabrication, or forestry capacity in this state, including through any of the following: (A) Direct use of mass timber products manufactured or fabricated in this state, where commercially available at the scale, specifications, and timeline required by the project. (B) Use of timber, lumber, or other wood products sourced from this state. (C) Partnerships, supply agreements, training programs, or other commitments that contribute to the future development of mass timber manufacturing, fabrication, or supply chain capacity in this state. (D) Documented efforts by the eligible applicant to identify and engage Michigan-based suppliers, fabricators, or manufacturers, even where in-state sourcing is not feasible for the project. (b) For a manufacturing and supply chain loan described in subsection (2)(c), all of the following, as applicable: (i) Whether the applicant will fill a needed gap in mass timber manufacturing or the mass timber supply chain in this state or otherwise advance the mass timber industry in this state. (ii) Whether the applicant will significantly contribute to carbon reduction. (iii) Whether the applicant will use wood sourced from sustainably managed forests. (iv) Whether the applicant will, to the extent commercially feasible, source wood and other materials and components from within this state, and the applicant's plan for increasing in-state sourcing over time. (5) In addition to the criteria under subsection (4), the fund may give preference to applicants or projects that will do 1 or more of the following, as considered and documented by the fund: (a) Provide affordable housing. (b) Revitalize a downtown site or brownfield site. As used in this subdivision: (i) "Brownfield site" means property included in a brownfield plan under the brownfield redevelopment financing act, 1996 PA 381, MCL 125.2651 to 125.2670. (ii) "Downtown site" means property located in either of the following: (A) A downtown area as that term is defined in section 58 of the state housing development authority act of 1966, 1966 PA 346, MCL 125.1458. (B) A downtown district as that term is defined in section 201 of the recodified tax increment financing act, 2018 PA 57, MCL 125.4201. (c) Support rural economic development. (6) If the fund determines, after the considerations under subsections (4) and (5), to provide a loan to an eligible applicant under the loan program, the fund shall enter into a written agreement with the eligible applicant that includes in a clear and concise manner all of the terms and conditions related to the loan, as determined and documented by the fund board, including, but not limited to, all of the following: (a) The total amount of the loan. (b) Subject to subsection (3)(e), the interest rate for the loan. (c) Subject to subsection (3)(f), the term of the loan. (d) Specific dates and benchmarks for the eligible applicant to receive the loan, including conditions for the disbursement of money in installments. (e) The purposes for which the loan proceeds may be used. (f) A provision that all money that is subject to clawback or required to be repaid under a specific repayment provision must be paid not later than 90 days after notification by the fund. Any amounts not paid within that 90-day period are subject to a penalty of 1% per month, prorated on a daily basis. (g) A provision that this state has a security interest as defined in section 1201 of the uniform commercial code, 1962 PA 174, MCL 440.1201, to the extent of the loan provided under the loan program. This subdivision does not apply if it conflicts with any contractual obligation of the eligible applicant or any federal or state bankruptcy or insolvency laws. (h) An audit provision that requires the fund to verify that the established benchmarks and other requirements have been met. (7) The fund shall post on its website or the website of the MEDC, not less than every 3 months, the name of each eligible applicant that received a loan under the loan program in that period, a description of the purpose of the loan, and the amount of the loan. (8) Payments of principal, interest, or penalties on loans under the loan program must be deposited in the mass timber revolving loan fund. (9) The fund board shall not disburse money from the loan program for a loan to an eligible applicant if that eligible applicant has not fully repaid all money subject to clawback or required to be repaid under a specific repayment provision as provided in any written agreement under this act or if that eligible applicant is in default on any grant, loan, or other economic assistance made or guaranteed by this state. All money paid to the fund pursuant to a clawback or specific repayment provision for a loan under the loan program must be deposited in the mass timber revolving loan fund. Any money allocated to the loan program that remains unexpended, unallocated, or unobligated at the end of the fiscal year reverts back to the mass timber revolving loan fund. Sec. 90w. (1) The mass timber advisory board is created in the fund. (2) The advisory board must consist of the following members: (a) The president of the fund or the president's designee. (b) The director of the department of natural resources or the director's designee. (c) The following 9 members appointed by the governor: (i) One member with expertise, knowledge, skill, or experience in forestry. (ii) One member with expertise, knowledge, skill, or experience in finance. (iii) One member with expertise, knowledge, skill, or experience in construction. (iv) One member with expertise, knowledge, skill, or experience in sustainability. (v) One member with expertise, knowledge, skill, or experience in manufacturing. (vi) One member with expertise, knowledge, skill, or experience in supply chains. (vii) Three members with expertise, knowledge, skill, or experience in mass timber. (3) The governor shall appoint the first members of the board within 90 days after the effective date of the amendatory act that added this section. (4) Of the first members appointed under subsection (2)(c), the governor shall appoint 3 of the first members to 1-year terms, 3 of the first members to 2-year terms, and 3 of the first members to 3-year terms. After the first appointments, the term of a member of the advisory board is 4 years or until a successor is appointed under subsection (2), whichever is later. (5) If a vacancy occurs on the advisory board, the governor shall appoint an individual to fill the vacancy for the balance of the term. (6) The governor may remove a member of the advisory board for incompetence, dereliction of duty, malfeasance, misfeasance, or nonfeasance in office, or any other good cause. (7) The president of the fund or the president's designee shall call the first meeting of the advisory board. At the first meeting, the advisory board shall elect a member as a chairperson and may elect other officers that it considers necessary or appropriate. The advisory board shall meet at least quarterly, or more frequently at the call of the chairperson or at the request of 5 or more members. (8) A majority of the members of the advisory board constitutes a quorum for transacting business. A vote in favor by a majority of the members of the advisory board serving is required for any action of the advisory board. (9) The advisory board shall conduct its business in compliance with the open meetings act, 1976 PA 267, MCL 15.261 to 15.275. (10) A writing that is prepared, owned, used, possessed, or retained by the advisory board in performing an official function is subject to the freedom of information act, 1976 PA 442, MCL 15.231 to 15.246. (11) A member of the advisory board is not entitled to compensation for service on the advisory board, but the advisory board may reimburse a member for actual and necessary expenses incurred in serving. (12) The advisory board shall do all of the following: (a) Advise and assist the fund with respect to the loan program. (b) Advise and assist the fund with respect to the technical assistance program. (13) The advisory board may do any of the following: (a) Assist the fund in promoting, encouraging, and facilitating the mass timber industry in this state. (b) Develop strategies and methods to do 1 or more of the following: (i) Attract mass timber manufacturers and related supply chain businesses to this state. (ii) Encourage the expansion of mass timber manufacturers and related supply chain businesses located in this state. (iii) Expand the use of mass timber for construction in this state. (iv) Otherwise develop the mass timber industry in this state. (c) Recommended tasks, activities, projects, and initiatives that would support the objectives of this chapter. (d) Provide other assistance or advice relating to the duties of the fund under this chapter as requested by the fund.
