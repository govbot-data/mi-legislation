SENATE BILL NO. 1211 A bill to amend 1976 PA 399, entitled "Safe drinking water act," by amending section 19 (MCL 325.1019), as amended by 2016 PA 478, and by adding section 19b. the people of the state of michigan enact: Sec. 19. (1) If Subject to the notice requirement under section 19b, if water delivered by or the operation of a public water supply is found not to be in compliance with the state drinking water standards, the department shall require the supplier of water to shall notify the department and its users of the extent and nature of the noncompliance. Notification of users must be in a form and manner prescribed or otherwise approved by the department. (2) In addition to the notification under subsection (1), if public education regarding lead is required under R 325.10410 of the Michigan Administrative Code, a supplier of water shall issue a public advisory within not later than 3 business days after the department notifies the supplier of water that an exceedance of the lead action level occurred. Additional public education tasks must be conducted as required under R 325.10410 of the Michigan Administrative Code. A supplier of water shall provide the public advisory under this subsection in a form and manner designed to fit the specific situation and the public advisory must be reasonably calculated to reach all persons served by the public water supply. To reach all persons served by the public water supply, a supplier of water shall use, at a minimum, 1 or more of the following forms of communicating the public advisory: (a) Appropriate broadcast media, such as radio and television. (b) Posting of the public advisory in conspicuous locations throughout the area served by the public water supply. (c) Hand delivering the public advisory to persons served by the public water supply. (d) A communication method other than one listed in subdivisions (a) to (c) as approved, in writing, by the department. (3) A notification or public advisory received pursuant to under this section or information obtained from the notification or public advisory shall must not be used against a person in a litigation, except a prosecution for perjury or for giving a false statement. (4) Not later than January 1, 2028, the department shall use and maintain a statewide computerized database that meets the following requirements: (a) Is publicly accessible. (b) Contains the following information for each supplier of water: (i) Each violation of or significant deficiency identified under this act, the rules promulgated under this act, or an order issued under this act that occurred on or after January 1, 2013, and whether the department is currently taking or previously took enforcement action against the supplier. (ii) The outcome and status, as applicable, of each violation disclosed under subparagraph (i). (iii) The results of any water sampled and analyzed under section 7. The results must be published not later than 1 month after the results are reported to the department. (c) Is searchable by community name and supplier of water. (5) As used in this section, "significant deficiency" means that term as described in R 325.10612(2)(c) of the Michigan Administrative Code. Sec. 19b. (1) Not later than 30 days after the department issues a notice of a significant deficiency for a violation under this act, the rules promulgated under this act, or an order issued under this act, the department shall hold a public hearing within the impacted community to explain the significant deficiency and identify concerns that need to be addressed through enforcement actions. (2) Not later than 30 days after the public hearing is held under subsection (1), the department, in consultation with the public water supply that was issued the notice of significant deficiency, shall provide a public notice that states the options the department and the public water supply are considering to resolve the significant deficiency and provide a public comment period of not less than 30 days.
