SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION NO. 16 SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION NO. 16 Senators Brinks and Nesbitt offered the following concurrent resolution: A concurrent resolution of tribute offered as a memorial for John Pappageorge, former member of the Michigan Senate and Michigan House of Representatives. Whereas, It was with great sorrow that the members of the Legislature learned about the passing of John Pappageorge. He will be remembered as a dedicated public servant by his constituents in the Detroit area and the people of our great state; and Whereas, Born July 19, 1931, and raised in Detroit, John Pappageorge entered military service shortly after graduating from high school. John Pappageorge retired from the United States Army as a Colonel, and was Airborne, Ranger, and Pathfinder qualified; and Whereas, After retiring from the military, John Pappageorge served as the director of business and strategic planning at General Dynamics Land Systems Division. However, it was in 1989 that he began his political career, serving on the Oakland County Board of Commissioners until 1992; and Whereas, In 1999, John Pappageorge joined the Michigan House of Representatives to represent the Forty-first District. While in the House, John Pappageorge served as the vice chair of the House Appropriations Subcommittee on State Police and Military Affairs, and as the chair of the Joint Committee on Administrative Rules and the House Appropriations Subcommittee on General Government. He was a member of the House committees on Employment Relations, Training and Safety; Judiciary; Senior Health, Security and Retirement; and Veterans Affairs and Homeland Security. John Pappageorge also served as a member of the House Appropriations subcommittees on Judiciary, Transportation, and Joint Capital Outlay; and Whereas, In 2007, John Pappageorge came back to Lansing after winning a competitive race to represent the Thirteenth District in the Michigan Senate. While in the Senate, he served as the chair of the Joint Committee on Administrative Rules and the Senate Appropriations subcommittees on General Government and Transportation. John Pappageorge was the vice chair of the Senate committees on Appropriations; Senior Citizens and Veterans Affairs; Veterans, Military Affairs and Homeland Security; and Infrastructure Modernization, as well as the Senate Appropriations subcommittees on Department of Community Health and State Police and Military Affairs. He also served as a member of the Senate committees on Homeland Security and Emerging Technologies, and Finance, as well as the Senate Appropriations subcommittees on Joint Capital Outlay; Federal Stimulus Oversight; and K-12, School Aid, and Education. John Pappageorge was also a member of the Senate Fiscal Agency Board of Governors and the Task Force on Transportation Infrastructure; and Whereas, While in Lansing, John Pappageorge was not afraid to make his voice heard and to stand up for the issues he believed would make the lives of everyday Michiganders better. These issues included government efficiency and road funding. John Pappageorge was also an early proponent of a new term-limit system for Michigan legislators, advocating for a combined system like the one we have now. In 2014, John Pappageorge was presented with the Distinguished Service Medal, recognizing his military and legislative service to the country and the state of Michigan; and Whereas, John Pappageorge is remembered fondly by many for his deep sense of integrity and loyalty, as well as his ability to always remain calm and level-headed. He was a true statesman and patriot who was honored to serve others. He is survived by his wife of nearly 29 years, Dianne; son, George; daughters, Christina and Melissa; one grandson; and three granddaughters.
