By December 1944, GM had secured the land in Warren, northeast of Detroit. Renowned architects Eliel Saarinen and his son, Eero Saarinen, served as architect-engineers for this historic project, with landscape design by Thomas Church; and Whereas, Official construction began on October 23, 1945, on a 320-acre site, with the General Motors Technical Center officially opening on May 16, 1956, hosting 5,000 distinguished guests; and Whereas, The facility quickly became a global hub for engineering, design, and research, helping to shape modern automotive advancement. The complex featured many innovative elements including an aluminum-domed auditorium used for hosting celebrations and events. On the west side of campus, an impressive fountain highlights a scenic 22-acre lake and 140-foot-tall stainless-steel spherical water tower capable of holding 250,000 gallons; and Whereas, For seventy years, the Global Technical Center has remained at the forefront of innovation, driving technological progress and supporting the growth of Michiganâs economy and workforce.
